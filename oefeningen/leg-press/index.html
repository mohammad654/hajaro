Leg Press

A stable machine press for heavy, high-volume leg training.

BeginnerMachine

How to perform the Leg Press

1 · Starting position

Sit with the back flat against the pad and feet shoulder-width apart in the middle of the platform. Release the safeties.

2 · Movement execution
  1. Lower the platform by bending the knees towards the chest.
  2. Stop before the lower back peels off the pad.
  3. Press back up without locking the knees aggressively.
3 · Ending position

Legs almost straight with knees soft.

Correct posture cues

  • Lower back stays on the pad
  • Knees track over toes
  • Control the bottom

Common mistakes

  • Lowering so deep the pelvis tucks under
  • Snapping the knees into hyperextension
  • Half reps with excessive load

Safety considerations

  • Always engage the safety stops when finished
