How coaching works

From intro to your first check-in

  1. Free intro

    We talk about your goal, your training history and your week. No pressure: you decide afterwards.

  2. Starting point

    A short questionnaire, starting measurements (photos only if you're comfortable with that) and a look at how you train and eat now.

  3. Your plan

    A training plan, and food targets if you want them, in your own client dashboard.

  4. Train, check in, adjust

    You log your sessions and send a weekly check-in. I look at it and change what isn't working.
