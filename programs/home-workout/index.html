About this program

A four-day upper/lower program for training at home with adjustable dumbbells, a resistance band and a bench. More reps and a slower tempo make light equipment properly challenging.

Who it's for

  • Anyone training at home
  • People who travel a lot or have busy jobs
  • Beginners who don't feel comfortable in a gym yet

How the training is set up

  • Upper / Lower / rest / Upper / Lower / rest / rest
  • Take 3 seconds to lower the weight on every rep

Warm-up

  1. 5 minutes of marching, jumping jacks or skipping
  2. Band pull-aparts and bodyweight squats
  3. 1 light set of the first exercise

Progression

  • Add reps first, then slow the tempo, then add weight
  • Once you do 3 or more reps above the top of the range, use heavier dumbbells or a harder variation

Rest days and recovery

  • Aim for 7 to 9 hours of sleep a night
  • Eat enough protein every day (usually 1.6 to 2.2 g per kg of body weight)
  • Keep rest days truly restful: light walking and mobility are ideal

Sets, reps and rest times are example ranges, not rules for everyone. Adjust them to your experience, recovery and body, or have them set for you through coaching. Stop any exercise that causes pain, and see a healthcare professional if needed.
