Muscle groups

Train every muscle group in balance

A balanced physique comes from training opposite muscle groups in proportion (chest and back, quads and hamstrings, biceps and triceps), and giving each one enough work every week to grow.

  • Aim for roughly 10 to 20 hard sets per muscle group per week
  • Train each muscle at least twice a week for most goals
  • Balance pushing with pulling to protect your shoulders
  • Train muscles that lag behind early in the session
