Muscle group reference

Train every muscle group with balance

A balanced physique comes from training opposing muscle groups in proportion — chest and back, quads and hamstrings, biceps and triceps — while giving each one enough weekly volume to grow.

  • Aim for roughly 10–20 hard sets per muscle group per week
  • Train each muscle at least twice per week for most goals
  • Pair pressing with pulling to protect your shoulders
  • Prioritise lagging muscle groups early in the session
