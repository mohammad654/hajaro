About this program

A four-day strength program built around the squat, bench press, deadlift and overhead press, with targeted extra exercises and short conditioning finishers.

Who it's for

  • Lifters who want to get measurably stronger
  • Athletes in sports where strength matters
  • Bodybuilders building a strength base in the off-season

How the training is set up

  • Two heavy lower-body days and two heavy upper-body days
  • Main lifts for 3 to 6 reps
  • Extra exercises for 8 to 12 reps
  • A 10-minute conditioning finisher twice a week

Warm-up

  1. 5 minutes of light cardio to raise body temperature
  2. Dynamic mobility for the joints you will train (hips, thoracic spine, shoulders)
  3. 2 or 3 warm-up sets, each heavier, for the first compound exercise
  4. 1 lighter feeler set before each new type of movement
  5. Work up to your first working set in 4 or 5 sets, each heavier

Progression

  • Main lifts: add 2.5 kg (upper body) or 5 kg (lower body) once you complete all the reps
  • If you miss reps two sessions in a row, take 10% off the weight and build back up
  • Every 6 to 8 weeks, or when progress stalls, take a lighter week with about half the sets

Rest days and recovery

  • Aim for 7 to 9 hours of sleep a night
  • Eat enough protein every day (usually 1.6 to 2.2 g per kg of body weight)
  • Keep rest days truly restful: light walking and mobility are ideal
  • Take your full rest on the main lifts: strength needs recovery between sets

Sets, reps and rest times are example ranges, not rules for everyone. Adjust them to your experience, recovery and body, or have them set for you through coaching. Stop any exercise that causes pain, and see a healthcare professional if needed.
