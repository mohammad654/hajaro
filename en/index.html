FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is coaching suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. Beginners often benefit the most from coaching because good technique and habits are built from the start. Your plan is matched to your current level and progresses as you improve.

How does online coaching work?

After a free consultation and onboarding questionnaire, you receive your training and nutrition plan in the client dashboard. You log your workouts, submit a weekly check-in with measurements and photos, and receive personal feedback and adjustments.

Do I need a gym membership?

Most programs are designed for a commercial gym, but home-based plans are available. Tell us what equipment you have and the plan is built around it.

How quickly will I see results?

Most clients notice strength and energy improvements within a few weeks and visible changes within 8–12 weeks. Results depend on consistency, starting point, nutrition, sleep and individual factors — no honest coach can guarantee a specific outcome.

Will I receive a meal plan?

You receive individual calorie and macronutrient targets with practical meal structure. Detailed meal plans are included in the Premium Transformation and Competition Preparation packages.

Can I pause or cancel?

Monthly packages can be cancelled with notice before the next billing date. Minimum terms, where they apply, are stated clearly on the pricing page and in the terms of service.

I have an injury or medical condition. Can I still be coached?

Please share any injuries or conditions in your consultation. Coaching is not a substitute for medical care — you may be asked to get clearance from a qualified healthcare professional before starting.

Do I need supplements?

No. Supplements are optional. Training, nutrition, sleep and consistency drive results. A small number of well-researched supplements, such as creatine monohydrate, may provide modest benefits — your coach can explain the evidence.
